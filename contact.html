Before you call

Handy things to have ready

Your last electricity bill

We need three numbers from it: your monthly units, your tariff per unit, and your sanctioned load. A photo on WhatsApp is fine.

A rough roof measurement

Length × breadth of the clear, shadow-free area. Even a pace-count helps us size the system before we visit.

Whether you want backup

Do you need power during outages? That single answer decides on-grid versus hybrid, and it changes the price significantly.
