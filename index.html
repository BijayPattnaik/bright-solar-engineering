Go green

Every roof that switches is coal that never gets burnt

India's grid still runs mostly on coal. A rooftop system does not just cut your bill — it takes your household's demand off that fire, every sunny day, for twenty-five years.

  • 4,380 units of clean electricity a year from a typical 3 kW home system — generated on your own roof, with no transmission losses.
  • 3.6 tonnes of CO₂ kept out of the air annually, using the Indian grid emission factor of 0.82 kg per unit.
  • 90 tonnes over the system's twenty-five-year life, allowing for the panels slowly losing output as they age.

Under PM Surya Ghar you pay the installer for the full system, and the central subsidy is credited by the government directly to your bank account after your system is commissioned and the net meter is installed — usually within 30 to 45 days. Be cautious of anyone who claims they can deduct the subsidy upfront on a residential system.
A standard on-grid system draws from the grid at night and exports surplus during the day, which your net meter credits against your bill. It shuts down during a power cut, by design, to protect linemen working on the line. If you need power during outages you need a hybrid system with a battery — we can quote that separately.
Roughly 90–100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW. So a 3 kW system needs about 300 sq ft. Shade matters more than area — a single water tank shadow falling across a string can cost you more generation than losing a whole panel.
Very little. Wash the panels with plain water every two to four weeks in dusty seasons — dust alone can cost 10–20% of your generation. There are no moving parts. The inverter is the component most likely to need attention over 25 years, and it typically carries a 5–10 year warranty.

DCR means Domestic Content Requirement — both the cells and the module are made in India. For subsidised residential systems under PM Surya Ghar, DCR modules are mandatory. Non-DCR panels can be cheaper or higher-spec, but they will disqualify your subsidy claim. Every panel in our catalogue is marked clearly.
