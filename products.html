Payment

You don't have to pay it all upfront

Bank finance available

You do not have to pay the full amount upfront. Solar systems are financed by most major banks, and we will help you put the file together.

  • Eligibility is decided by the bank, as per its own norms — income, credit history, documents and sanction limits are all theirs to assess
  • Collateral-free loans are available for residential systems up to 3 kW under the PM Surya Ghar scheme, at concessional rates
  • For many households the monthly instalment is smaller than the electricity bill the system replaces
  • We prepare the quotation, specification sheet and technical documents your bank asks for
  • The government subsidy still comes to you separately, by direct benefit transfer, after commissioning

    Choosing help

    Not sure which one is right?

    Three questions decide it for almost everyone.

    Are you claiming the subsidy?

    If yes, you must use a DCR module. That narrows the list immediately — look for the green DCR tag. Waaree's entire range qualifies; Waaree and V-Guard have specific DCR models.

    How much roof do you have?

    Tight roof, higher bill? Go for the highest-wattage, highest-efficiency panel you can afford — a 580 W TOPCon fits more kW into the same area than a 400 W Mono PERC. Plenty of space? Mono PERC gives you more kW per rupee.

    How long will you own the property?

    Staying twenty-plus years? TOPCon's slower degradation and 30-year warranty are worth the premium. Shorter horizon, or a rental? Mono PERC recovers its cost faster.
