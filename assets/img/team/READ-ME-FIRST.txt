PUT YOUR PHOTO IN THIS FOLDER
=============================

A real face matters on a local trade website. Someone spending a lakh
or more on a 25-year product wants to see who they are buying from.

HOW TO ADD IT
-------------
1. Copy your photo into this folder.
   Name it simply, no spaces:   ardhendu-pattnaik.jpg

2. Open  assets/js/config.js  and find the "team" section.
   Remove the // in front of the example lines and edit them:

     items: [
       { src: 'assets/img/team/ardhendu-pattnaik.jpg',
         alt: 'Ardhendu Pattnaik, Partner at Bright Solar Engineering',
         name: 'Ardhendu Pattnaik',
         role: 'Partner',
         focus: '50% 20%',
         line: 'The person who answers the phone and turns up for the survey.' },
     ]

3. Save. Done. The section appears on the About page.

Until you add one, the whole section stays hidden - visitors never
see an empty space or a broken image.


TWO PARTNERS - THE PHOTOS MUST MATCH
------------------------------------
There are two cards now, side by side:

  ardhendu-pattnaik.jpg   (already in place)
  subhendu-pattnaik.jpg   (STILL NEEDED)

Because they sit next to each other, they have to be shot the same
way or the pair looks wrong. Both should be HEAD AND SHOULDERS, from
roughly the same distance, facing the camera.

A full-length photo will NOT work here. The site crops to a small
circle, so cropping a face out of a standing shot leaves a soft,
low-resolution head next to a sharp one. If the only photo you have
is full-length, take a new close one - it is thirty seconds and the
difference is obvious.

Until subhendu-pattnaik.jpg exists, only Ardhendu's card shows and
the layout returns to the single wide version. Nothing breaks.


BEST RESULT: CROP IT SQUARE FIRST
---------------------------------
The site crops your photo to a circle. If the original is a full
standing shot, the circle ends up mostly shirt with a small face.

Before saving, crop the photo to a SQUARE around the head and
shoulders - top of the hair near the top edge, cut around chest
level. Windows Photos does this in seconds: open the photo, click
Crop, choose the 1:1 / Square ratio, drag, save.

Do that and you can leave `focus` as it is.


ABOUT THE BACKGROUND
--------------------
The photo is cropped to a CIRCLE at head-and-shoulders. That alone
removes most of a busy shop background - shelving, boxes, stock - so
in many cases you do not need to edit the photo at all. Try it first.

If you do want the background removed properly, it takes 30 seconds
and costs nothing:

  - remove.bg           upload, download, done
  - Canva               Edit image > Background Remover
  - Microsoft Paint     Windows 11 has "Remove background" built in
  - Photos app          Windows 11, right-click the subject

Save the result as a PNG if the background is transparent, or put a
plain white or light grey behind it. Either looks correct on the site.


IF THE CROP LOOKS WRONG
-----------------------
Change the second number in `focus` in config.js. Only that one.

  '50% 10%'   crop higher - use if there is too much space above the head
  '50% 20%'   default - suits most head-and-shoulders photos
  '50% 35%'   crop lower - use if the top of the head is cut off


WHAT MAKES A GOOD PHOTO
-----------------------
- Head and shoulders, camera at eye level, not looking down at you
- Face the camera squarely and hold the phone level
- Daylight if possible - near a window or door, not under a tube light
- Plain wall behind you if you can manage it
- No flash if you can avoid it. Flash flattens the face and throws a
  hard shadow onto the wall behind.
- Ask someone else to take it. An arm's-length selfie distorts a face.

Shown at 190px across, so any modern phone photo is more than enough.
If the file is over about 2 MB, resize it so the site stays fast on
mobile data.
