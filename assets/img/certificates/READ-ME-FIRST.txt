PUT YOUR DEALERSHIP CERTIFICATES IN THIS FOLDER
===============================================

Scans or clear photos of your authorisation letters from
Waaree and V-Guard.

Anyone can type "authorised dealer" on a website. Showing the
actual paperwork is what separates you from the people who
just claim it.

HOW TO ADD THEM
---------------
1. Copy the scans into this folder:
     waaree.jpg, vguard.jpg

2. Open  assets/js/config.js  and find the "credentials" section.
   Remove the // and edit:

     items: [
       { src: 'assets/img/certificates/waaree.jpg',
         alt: 'Waaree authorised dealer certificate',
         label: 'Waaree Energies' },
     ]

3. Save. The section appears on the About page.

Until you add at least one, the section stays hidden.

BEFORE YOU UPLOAD - CHECK EACH SCAN
-----------------------------------
These go on a public website. Look at each one and cover or blur
anything you do not want public, such as bank account numbers or
personal ID numbers. Your firm name, the brand, the dealership
period and the signature are the parts that matter.
