AssumptionValue usedWhy
Daily generation4 units per kW per dayA realistic annual average across most of India, allowing for monsoon and winter months.
System cost₹72,000/kW at 1 kW, falling to ₹58,000/kW at 3 kW and ₹48,000/kW at 10 kW+Larger systems cost less per kW — the inverter, structure and labour are shared across more panels.
Roof area90 sq ft per kWStandard spacing for shadow-free mounting with walkway access.
Panel degradation0.5% per yearTypical linear warranty rate. TOPCon panels degrade slower than this.
Tariff inflation4% per yearConservative. Indian retail tariffs have historically risen faster in many states.
CO₂ factor0.82 kg per unitApproximate emission intensity of the Indian grid.
Central subsidy₹30,000/kW first 2 kW, ₹18,000 for the 3rdPM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, capped at ₹78,000.
Odisha state subsidyup to ₹60,000 for systems up to 3 kWOdisha Akshaya Shakti Bikash Yojana (OASBY). Confirm the current slab at application.
About the Odisha state subsidy: the state has committed to additional assistance of up to ₹60,000 for residential systems up to 3 kW, on top of the central amount. Scheme slabs and budgets are revised from time to time, so we confirm the exact figure that applies to your application with your discom before it goes in your quotation. See the full Odisha rules →
What this calculator does not know: how much shade falls on your roof, which direction it faces, your discom's slab structure, whether your sanctioned load allows the system size, or the price of the specific panels and inverter you choose. Those can move the numbers meaningfully in either direction — which is why the site survey is free.
